# Third-party notices

Jambul doesn't include any third-party code yet.

When code under a permissive license, such as MIT, BSD or Apache-2.0, is adapted into Jambul, its copyright notice and license text go here, along with the files it went into. Code under the GPL or other copyleft licenses is never copied into Jambul.

## Third-party marks and icons

Jambul doesn't bundle, redistribute or show third-party logos. It marks each AI tool with a symbol of its own: the tool's initial in a color, or `>_` for Codex.

Third-party product names identify the tools referenced in Jambul and belong to their respective owners. Jambul is an independently developed macOS utility and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Apple, Anthropic, Google, or OpenAI.

If you represent a rights holder and have a concern about how your product is referenced, open an issue or write to the address in `SECURITY.md`.
